Terms of order

Our online shop opens soon; until then, you can’t order yet. This is how ordering from Cascarup will work: you order online, pay with TWINT or card, and we post your bottles to you.

Seller

Cascarup, student company in the Company Programme of Young Enterprise Switzerland, c/o Collège Saint-Michel, Fribourg. Email: info@cascarup.ch

Products and prices

We sell our syrup in a 200 ml hip flask and in a 500 ml bottle, while stocks last. Prices are in Swiss francs:

  • Original: 200 ml CHF 6.95, 500 ml CHF 12.95
  • Cozy Spice: 200 ml CHF 7.95, 500 ml CHF 13.95

Shipping

We deliver within Switzerland, by Swiss Post as PostPac Economy. Shipping costs depend on the weight of the parcel and are shown in the basket before you pay:

  • up to 2 kg: CHF 9.00
  • up to 10 kg: CHF 12.00
  • up to 30 kg: CHF 21.00

We send your order as soon as it has been paid; Swiss Post expects delivery to take two working days.

Ordering and payment

You put the bottles in your basket, enter your delivery address and choose TWINT or card. Before you pay, you see your order, the shipping costs and the total, and you can change anything. “Pay now” takes you to the secure payment page of our payment provider Stripe. The purchase is concluded when your payment goes through; you receive a confirmation by email with your order number.

Changes and cancellation

Until we have sent your order, you can change or cancel it free of charge: write to us at info@cascarup.ch and give your order number. We will refund the amount you paid by the same method.

Food

Ingredients, allergens and shelf life are listed in the shop under “Ingredients and notes” and on the label. Our syrup contains caffeine from the coffee cherry. If a bottle arrives damaged or something is wrong with it, get in touch, ideally with a photo; we will replace it or refund the price.

Privacy

How we handle your details is explained in our privacy policy.
