The terms
  • A participation certificate costs CHF 15.–. Online, you can sign up for up to 20 certificates at once.
  • Issued by Cascarup, a student company in the Company Programme of Young Enterprise Switzerland, c/o Collège Saint-Michel, Fribourg.
  • The certificate is valid for our 2026/27 company year.
  • If you choose “Money back”, we’ll pay your amount back to you at the end of the company year. We’ll email you in good time with exactly how and when.
  • If you choose “Keep it”, your contribution stays with Cascarup and you keep the certificate as a keepsake.
  • We need your details to match your certificate to you and to reach you at the end. More in our privacy policy.
  • Questions? Write to us at info@cascarup.ch.
